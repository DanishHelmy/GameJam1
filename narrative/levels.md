# SUBJECT 0 - World, Levels and Limbs (v0.3)

Follows your sketch: 6 levels, one new limb per level, each level solved with
the limbs gathered so far. Replaces the 5-chapter table in `story.md` for the jam build.

## World: Meridian City
Meridian was a "city of the future". The Halden Project turned dying patients into
Subjects: body replaced piece by piece with machine parts. Each Subject was
"upgraded" until no human choices were left. Then the Project was shut down and the
city evacuated overnight. The city AI (the Announcer) still loops its cheerful message.

Tommy (Subject 0) was the first patient. His body would not accept the machine, so the
Project took his best parts back, stored them in the tower, and marked him REJECTED.
Every limb he finds is his own, kept in a labelled case. Each one carries a small
memory-echo of the house (a door, a window, a voice).

**Colour as progress:** Level 1 is grey. Each limb adds one warm accent (a lit
window, a yellow door, a warm lamp). The ending is fully warm.

## Difficulty curve
1. One idea each, shown before tested. 2. Test with small risk. 3. Combine with older limbs.
Later levels always require earlier limbs (arm throw to hold a switch while jumping, x-ray
to find the grapple anchor, dash after a swing).

## Boss: THE UPGRADE
The version of Tommy the Project wanted: all machine, no hesitation. It copies
the player's limbs one by one. Defeat it by using each ability in turn on its three
weak points. It does not die; when it stops, it says the one word it was given: "Subject."

## Art direction (for the artist)
- Pixel art, 32x32 hero sprite, 16x16 tiles. Palette: slate greys + one warm accent per level.
- Hero grows on screen: torso+head (L1), +leg (L2), etc. One sprite sheet per limb state.
- Limb cases: glass tubes with a handwritten label ("T. - left leg").
- The house: a small recurring doodle on walls, files and cases. Same 8x8 icon each time.
- Memory scene: the only full-colour, softer, painted-style image.
