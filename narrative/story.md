# Story Bible (DRAFT v0.2)

Built on the team design doc (`docs/design.md`, branch `mechanics/design-doc`).
Items marked **(?)** are my guesses - please confirm or change.

## One line
A rejected machine climbs a dead city toward a signal, and at the top finds the
one thing it was never supposed to keep: a memory of being human.

## Tone
Sad, quiet, gentle. Few words. The world does the talking (logs, empty pods,
a looping announcement). Small moments of warmth, never jokes at the hero's expense.

## Theme
What remains of a person after the body is replaced. Being thrown away is not
the same as being worthless.

## The hero - "Subject 0" (?)
~80% machine. Has no name, only the label on the patient file: **SUBJECT 0 -
REJECTED**. Doesn't speak; thoughts appear as short lines of text. Name is
a reveal at the end (see Ending) (?)

**What "rejected" means:** the program replaced a dying person's body with
machine parts. Most subjects were "upgraded" until nothing human was left and
kept. Subject 0 is the one the program marked **REJECTED** - the body and the
machine never fully agreed, the human memory would not delete, so the project
stripped the best parts (a leg and an arm) and left the rest to be scrapped.
Then the district was evacuated and the lab forgotten. (?)

## Setting
A ruined downtown after a quiet evacuation. Grey concrete, dead neon, rain,
overgrown lots. One lit window on the central tower: the signal. Pixel-art
backgrounds (per the team sketches).

## Recurring elements
- **The announcement:** a looping city-AI voice, cheerful and wrong. Changes
  slightly each chapter as the hero gets closer to the tower.
- **The drone (from Skyway):** the only other working thing. Kind, small, talks.
  Doesn't know everything. (?) name: **Pip**
- **Data logs:** 2-3 hidden per chapter. They tell the evacuation and experiment story.
- **The house:** a small drawing / photo / door keeps appearing in lab and
  subject files. The hero never understands why it matters. It pays off in the ending.

## Chapters

|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | The Lab | Movement, spring jump, detach arm | Wakes missing a leg and arm. Patient file: REJECTED. Escapes. |
| 1 | Street Level | Spring pads | Announcement loops. First sight of the tower. |
| 2 | Collapsed Subway | Detach puzzles | Dead cyborgs. Their logs: "upgraded past the point of being human." The hero sees what it could have become. |

| 4 | The Tower Core | All abilities | The signal is an "all clear" that was never sent. The climb. The ending. |

## Ending - "The House"
At the top, the transmitter is waiting. The hero can send the all clear, but
nothing happens - the city is already empty. The signal was never for the city.

Instead the terminal plays the one memory the program could not delete:
the screen fades out of pixel art into warmer, softer colour. A **human**
(the hero, before) stands in front of a small house at dusk. A door, a lit
window, someone calling a name. The hero finally hears their own name.

Cut back: the hero stands alone on the roof, still a machine, but the
tower lights come on one by one across the city - not a rescue, only proof
that someone was here. Last line: the name. Fade out. (?)

No choice at the end (jam scope): the ending is the memory.
